## Voice snapshot (as of 2026-05-30, 3 essays in the published archive, 1 written under 1A)

### Core register
**Reflective storyteller, not explainer.** Shows a scene, lets the reader sit in it, then names what he felt — trusts the reader to feel the point rather than announcing it. This is the load-bearing calibration; the dominant failure mode is drift toward explainer/thought-leader voice.

### Signature moves (each cited)
- **Self-Q&A rhythm** — poses the skeptical question, answers it terse. *"So do I believe…? Right now, yes."* / *"I am going to apply? Yeh."* (FIJC close). The strongest positional-voice device.
- **Pull-quote interlocutor** — a skeptical fake-reader question as a blockquote to pivot between beats. *"But why?"* (You're Not Boring). Sparingly — 2–4 per post.
- **Standalone aphorism as section landing** — one bold/italic line alone after a build-up. *"The riskiest thing you can do in a casino is to win."* These are the screenshot lines. 2–4 per post.
- **Strikethrough self-deprecation** — `~~gambler~~ risk taker`. Same self-undersell shape as *"young dashing engineer"*, *"stupid sexy NVIDIA"*, *"dumbass"* (across FIJC, You're Not Boring). The "complain" gear from the working beat. One or two per post.
- **Pop-culture pastiche** — Bond pastiche, Dune *~spice~*, South Park *"stupid sexy Nvidia"*, *"cloud boomers"*, *"throw money at locals"* (FIJC). Voice marker that lands the lens by P2.
- **Short paragraphs**, often one sentence — white space does work.
- **Em-dashes for pivots, italics for stress.** Bold is rare (AI prose over-bolds; he doesn't).
- **Light conversational section headers** as scene breaks — *"How did I get here?"*, *"The Outcome"*. Never *"Key Takeaways"* / *"Conclusion."*
- **Philosophical pivot near the end** — swerve from the concrete anecdote into a broader claim about agency/identity/how to live. The move that lifts a post from "thing that happened" to "thing worth reading."
- **Honest scope-narrowing as a voice move** — *"I don't have much to talk about on the resilience pillar…"* (FIJC) — named in the log as one of his strongest moves.

### Tone calibration
- **Australian English** — *realised, haemorrhaging, aye, Yeh.* Never normalise to American.
- First person, conversational; contractions fine, not forced.
- Self-aware, not self-important — when a claim sounds grand, the next line punctures it.
- Asserts, then earns it. No corporate hedging.
