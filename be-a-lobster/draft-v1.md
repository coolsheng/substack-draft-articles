# Be a Lobster

Do you know that lobsters grow all their life? In fact, they are immortal if the environment allows them to be.

The problem is, though, that the shell of the lobster does not grow with it. That means as the lobster ages, it will start to push against its shell. It will start to experience discomfort, pressure, and a call to change.

Eventually, the lobster will have to choose - either stay in its current shell or grow out of it but become vulnerable to the elements.

It's during this season that the lobster is both at its weakest but also at its moment of greatest potential - a pause to before the next version of itself.

In case you haven't been following the metaphor, this is a common impasse each of us will face in our lives. It's often at this stage that we start to question ourselves whether we're in the right place, we feel weak, vulnerable, and even lost.

You can't force or fast track this process. You just have to sit there and wait for this new shell, new armour to grow into place. It's only when it's ready that you will find the resilience to tackle the next chapter of your life.

So don't rush, take your time to grow and be a lobster.
